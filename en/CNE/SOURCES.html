CNE — sources

Unpublished tool: what the literature search looked for, did not find, and the neighborhood gathered instead.

bibliography

Unpublished tool. After checking, no publication describes CNE, nor the two fine-tuned models it downloads. The acronym Clinical Notes Extraction itself appears nowhere outside the repository.

The fine-tuning protocol, the TMJ/Ortho annotation corpus, and the evaluation thus remain undeterminable, exactly as CNE.md already noted. The only documented tie is the MedX → CNE lineage, and MedX/SOURCES.md shows that MedX's paper describes a BART, not a Llama or a Qwen: it does not cover CNE.

Neighborhood

None of these works is about CNE. They share neither the authors, nor the models, nor the corpus. They are gathered here because they describe the same technical gesture — running an open-weight LLM locally, without sending the patient's text to a third-party service, to extract structured fields from it — and they make it possible to assess what CNE does, in the absence of any text about CNE itself.

  • Wiest 2024 (file above): the work closest to CNE's gesture. Open-weight LLMs deployed locally extract structured data from clinical reports, with the privacy argument used to justify the local deployment. It is the same setup, in a different domain and with different authors.
  • Panchal 2026 and Dorémus 2025 (files above): the de-identification of clinical notes by LLM, respectively as a benchmark and as a real deployment in an emergency department. CNE de-identifies nothing — that is precisely the question these two articles ask and that the repository does not address.
  • Nahm WJ, Yin ES, Milam EC, Weed JG. Local Deployment of Open-Weight Language Models in Dermatology: Viewpoint on Privacy, Equity, and Practical Implementation. JMIR Dermatol 2026;9:e94764, 10.2196/94764, PMC13496321, open access. A viewpoint on what the local deployment of an open-weight model in a clinical specialty actually entails in practice.

Reminder, as the tool page already noted: do not tie CNE to this literature. Nothing in the repository indicates that the dcbia/* models come from published work, and these articles say nothing about CNE's corpus, annotation protocol, or performance.

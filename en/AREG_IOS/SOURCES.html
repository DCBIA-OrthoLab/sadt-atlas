AREG_IOS — sources

Bibliography for AREG_IOS: what has been deposited in this folder, what remains paywalled with no author version, and the methodological neighborhood that does not describe this code.

AREG_IOS conda env: shapeaxi Pipeline page

The tool is published, once: AReg IOS: Automatic Registration on IntraOralScans (ShapeMI 2023, LNCS 14350). The paper describes the automatic palatal path. This repository's code departs from it on two points flagged on the pipeline page: the initial alignment by crown centroids is moved upstream into PRE_ASO_IOS + StartByMatchingCentroidsOn(), and the MGL path (mucogingival line) appears nowhere in the paper — it's a later internal development, not published. The reference paper is paywalled at Springer and has no deposited author version (Unpaywall: closed, no copy on deposit; nothing in PMC, Europe PMC, HAL; Deep Blue inaccessible to crawlers, to be checked by hand).

AREG_IOSCBCT — sources

No publication describes this module. What is published are the upstream building blocks that the orchestrator stacks in front of the CLI.

Verification

The sheet said so, and the verification confirms it: neither Crossref, nor OpenAlex, nor Europe PMC, nor arXiv, nor HAL, nor NA-MIC project weeks 36 through 43 contain anything about an IOS↔CBCT registration by occlusal landmarks + ICP on an isosurface signed by this group. The two NA-MIC projects titled "Multimodal Registration" (PW40 2024, PW41 2024) deal with MRI↔CBCT, not IOS↔CBCT.

What is published are the upstream building blocks that the orchestrator stacks in front of the CLI: ALI_CBCT for the volumetric landmarks, DentalModelSeg for the crowns. The CLI itself (Procrustes + ICP + marching cubes at threshold 400) has no paper.

The first four are the pipeline's building blocks, not the pipeline itself:

  • Gillot 2023 describes ALI_CBCT, which supplies the 12 occlusal landmarks on the volume side (steps 3 and 7 of Fully-Automated mode);
  • Fly-by-CNN then DentalModelSeg describe CrownSegmentationcli, which sets the Universal_ID array on the IOS — here only to let ALI_IOS aim its cameras tooth by tooth;
  • Anchling 2023 is the AREG paper on the CBCT side: same family, same original repository, but mono-modal CBCT→CBCT registration. It does not describe the multimodal case.

The last two are neighborhood work (see further below), kept here because they are the only complete, freely readable descriptions of the IOS↔CBCT problem.

Neighborhood

Work that addresses the same problem but does not describe this code.

  • Kim 2023 (2023_Kim_registration_CBCT_intraoral_scan.pdf): same objective, but with tooth segmentation on both sides, then RANSAC + ICP. Reported error 0.234 ± 0.019 mm. AREG_IOSCBCT takes the lighter path: landmarks instead of volumetric segmentation, thresholded isosurface instead of segmented teeth.
  • Jang 2024 (2024_Jang_CBCT_intraoral_impressions_integration.pdf): tooth-by-tooth identification and correction of the impression's stitching error. Fully learned pipeline, unrelated to the implementation here.
  • Zheng 2025 (systematic review, above): general context.
  • ALI_IOS, which supplies the 12 occlusal landmarks on the mesh side (step 8), has no publication. Its only public description is the NA-MIC Project Week 36 (2022) page "ALIIOS — Automatic Landmarks Identification for Intra Oral Scans" (Baquero, Gillot, Cevidanes, Prieto): projectweek.na-mic.org, which itself cites no reference.

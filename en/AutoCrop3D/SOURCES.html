AutoCrop3D — sources

AutoCrop3D bibliography: nothing describes it, two articles cite it in a single sentence.

unpublished AutoCrop3D page

Unpublished. No article, abstract, poster, or Project Week report describes AutoCrop3D. Two articles cite it in passing as a tool they used, one sentence each, without describing either how it works or its parameters; they are retrieved here because they are the only published trace of the module's existence. The documentation remains that of the repository and that of Slicer's Crop Volume module, whose semantics AutoCrop3D reuses.

Neighboring resources

These resources do not describe AutoCrop3D; they document what it reuses the semantics of, or what it works around.

  • Crop Volume — Slicer documentation: the reference for the expected behavior (ROI, spacing scale, interpolation, isotropic).
  • Crop Volume Sequence — Slicer documentation: cited by the repository's README as the limitation AutoCrop3D works around (files too heavy to load as a sequence).
  • DCBIA-OrthoLab/SlicerAutomatedDentalTools: the module's repository. The CLI's documentation-url, meanwhile, points to Jeanneclre/DCBIA-code, the author's personal repository, which is not the project's own.

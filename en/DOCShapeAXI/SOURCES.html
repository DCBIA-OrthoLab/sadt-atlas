DOCShapeAXI — sources

Bibliography for the tool: what is deposited in this folder, what remains to be checked online, and the neighborhood of publications that do not describe the module itself.

← DOCShapeAXI

A partially published tool, and published in separate pieces: no paper describes the Slicer module, but each of the three embedded datasets has its own clinical publication, and the shapeaxi framework has its own as well.

Code / paper divergence

The code diverges from its reference paper. The 2024 SPIE paper (2024_Prieto_ShapeAXI_SPIE) describes a multi-view render over an icosahedron subdivision, with the images processed by a ResNet-18 followed by additive attention — a single branch. The checkpoints actually downloaded by the module are SaxiMHAFB* models: a 4096-point point-cloud branch with KNN attention plus a 42-view / ImageNet-pretrained EfficientNet-B0 branch, concatenated. This two-branch architecture appears nowhere in the SPIE paper; it is described, as such, in Mattos 2025 (2025_Mattos_adenoid_airway_obstruction, § Deep learning model). The SPIE paper's figures are therefore not those of the distributed models.

A second, subtler divergence: the "condyles" experiment in the SPIE paper is a binary classification (healthy / Degenerative Joint Disease, 90 subjects, 79.78%), whereas the shipped checkpoint is condyles_4_class — 4 severity classes. No text found describes this 4-class checkpoint. The SPIE paper gives 81.58% for clefts on 4 severity classes, and there the dataset does match (see Miranda 2023 below, same cohort).

Verified

Correction to the page: contrary to what DOCShapeAXI.html stated, a publication dedicated to the Alveolar Bone Defect in Cleft dataset does exist — Miranda et al., Sci Rep 2023, which defines the 0–3 severity index and the SurfGradCAM explainability on 194 CLP patients.

Neighborhood

These works do not describe DOCShapeAXI. They are listed here because they set the condyle severity scale that the condyles_4_class dataset reuses without any text documenting it for this checkpoint.

  • Tubau Ribera 2019 and de Dumast 2018 (SPIE + CMIG), above: the Shape Variation Analyzer lineage, the direct ancestor of the group's condyle classifier. They grade condylar degeneration into 5, 6, or 7 groups depending on the version (normal, near-normal, overgrowth, Degeneration 1–4), on geometric descriptors + heat kernel signature, not on multi-view rendering. None of the three produces 4 classes, and none uses the architecture shipped here.
  • Gomes LR, Gomes M, Jung B, Paniagua B, Ruellas AC, Gonçalves JR, Styner MA, Wolford L, Cevidanes L. Diagnostic index of three-dimensional osteoarthritic changes in temporomandibular joint condylar morphology. J Med Imaging 2015;2(3):034501, 10.1117/1.JMI.2.3.034501, PMC4495313. The clinical diagnostic index that the whole SVA lineage descends from. Not freely downloadable.
  • Grad-CAM (file above): the original explainability method. The module applies its "SurfGradCAM" variant via Captum, on a mesh rather than an image; SurfGradCAM is described in Miranda 2023 and Mattos 2025, not in Selvaraju.

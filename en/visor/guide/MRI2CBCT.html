MRI2CBCT: not available with VISOR

MRI2CBCT cannot be run through a VISOR server today. This page says what that means for you. The legacy module has its own page.

VISOR: not ported Legacy page Atlas (VISOR)

MRI2CBCT is not available with VISOR. The server has no MRI2CBCT tool, and the VISOR client does not ship the module: its description says it is "temporarily not shipped until it is ported to the server" (AutomatedDentalToolsRemote CMakeLists.txt:10,31-32, commit 19b95e8). The current version, the one-button rewrite described in the legacy guide, is ported nowhere. The VISOR extension has no MRI2CBCT panel at all.

To register an MRI onto a CBCT, use the legacy extension on your own computer. Your files then stay on your machine. See When to keep the legacy modules.

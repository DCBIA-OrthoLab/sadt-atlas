AREG_CBCT with VISOR

What changes when you run AREG_CBCT through a VISOR server from 3D Slicer: what to provide, what is the same, what is different. The legacy module has its own page.

VISOR Legacy page Atlas (VISOR)

AREG_CBCT registers a follow-up CBCT (T2) onto the baseline CBCT (T1) of the same patient, on a region that has not changed between the two. With VISOR the registration is computed on a server: in Slicer you fill a form, send the folders and get the results back. What you do is the same in spirit, but the panel and a few details differ. This page was written from the VISOR code (SADT-VISOR 6ba2a78, VISOR-serve e9a7e5d, AutomatedDentalToolsRemote 19b95e8); the how and why are on the Atlas page.

What changes in the panel

  • Open the module AREG Remote (category “Automated Dental Tools Remote”). One module serves the three kinds of AREG registration: the first field, Mode, is where you pick CBCT. The other fields then follow the mode.
  • Automation keeps the three choices (Semi-Automated, Fully-Automated, Oriented + Fully-Automated) and starts on Fully-Automated.
  • Nothing to download: the segmentation and orientation models stay on the server, and the field for the segmentation model is not shown.
  • The review pauses (“Review and manual adjustment”), the Merge Segmentations box and Include Approximation Step are not in the panel.
  • The check box Load the results into the scene when done is ticked by default. It loads the registered volumes, never the .tfm, and loads nothing at all when more than 12 files come back.

What you provide

  • T1 and T2: a folder each, or a hosted test cohort chosen from the list above the pickers. A single cohort folder holding T1/ and T2/ is accepted. Accepted formats: .nii.gz .nrrd.gz .gipl.gz .nii .nrrd .gipl. Tick Input is DICOM for DICOM series.
  • Register on: the regions, as before. Each one gives its own registration and its own output folder.
  • In Semi-Automated, the T1 masks, which are required. A mask is recognised when its name holds a mask word (mask, seg, pred or segmentation) and a region word as separate parts of the name.
  • In Oriented + Fully-Automated, the Orientation reference, which is required.
  • AMASSS segmentation (automatic modes): ticks the structures you also want back to look at. They do not change what the registration uses.

What stays the same

A rigid elastix registration, confined to the region you chose on T1, with the T2 moved into the T1 frame. For each patient and region you get the registered T2 and its .tfm. Your inputs are not modified.

What is different in the results

  • The results are in one folder per region code (CB, MAND, MAX), plus AREG_report.json at the root: for each patient and region, whether it worked, which mask was used, and a note when something was unusual.
  • The written transform now includes the centre of rotation elastix used. The legacy module dropped it, so its transform could differ from the registration elastix had actually found, by an amount that grows with the distance of the scan from the origin; the Atlas page gives the figures.
  • T2 is no longer centred beforehand, and in Oriented mode only T1 is oriented.
  • Only T1 is segmented: the segmentations of the registered T2 that the legacy module could return are not produced.
  • A subject found in only one of the two folders is not dropped silently: it is listed in the report, and a run where nothing pairs stops with an error that explains the pairing rule.
Oriented mode: choose the Frankfurt reference

The orientation step asks ASO for its default landmarks, the Frankfurt set, whatever reference you picked. The Occlusal reference carries other landmarks, and ASO refuses a combination it cannot support. If you pick the Occlusal reference in this mode, expect that refusal. This was read in the code; the chain was not run.

AutoMatrix — sources

AutoMatrix's bibliography: search done, negative result — no work describes or cites the tool.

unpublished Pipeline page

Unpublished, and nothing cites it. No paper, abstract, poster, thesis or Project Week report covers AutoMatrix, and none names it either. It's a batch-application utility written for the lab (Gaëlle Leroux, UoM/DCBIA): the scientific content is entirely in the modules that produce the matrices — AREG, ASO, MRI2CBCT, FlexReg — and is covered in their respective pages.

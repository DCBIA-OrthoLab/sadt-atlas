MRI2CBCT

Rigid multimodal MRI → CBCT registration of the TMJ by elastix (Mattes mutual information), at the end of five independent preparation steps; the only network segments the condyle on the CBCT to provide an anchor point — it registers nothing by itself.

Leroux 2024 · CLIP, LNCS 15196 6 MRI2CBCT_* CLIs Slicer's Python, no conda Bibliography

Rigid multimodal MRI → CBCT registration of the TMJ. Six independent steps, each with its own button and CLI, ending in a rigid elastix registration by mutual information. The module's only network is an nnU-Net that segments the condyle on the CBCT: it takes no part in the registration itself, it provides an anchor point and a crop box.

1. Where it sits in the pipeline

Nothing in the repository calls MRI2CBCT, and MRI2CBCT calls no other module in the extension. It declares itself dependent on SlicerNNUNet (MRI2CBCT.py:150) but never imports it: it is the nnUNetv2_predict executable that gets launched as a subprocess.

CalledHowWhen
6 repository MRI2CBCT_* CLIsslicer.cli.runone per button
nnUNetv2_predictsubprocess.run from the CLIApproximate and TMJ Crop
slicer.modules.fiducialregistrationslicer.cli.runSyncend of automatic approximation

The comment at the top of MRI2CBCT_CLI_utils/__init__.py claims that resample_images is consumed by AREG_IOSCBCT and VFACE: a grep across the whole repository finds no caller outside MRI2CBCT_CLI. The lazy import is still useful (it avoids pulling in torchreg for a plain resample), but the stated justification no longer holds.

3. Pipeline order, and what is actually mandatory

[MRI orientation/centering] → [L/R crop] → resample → approximation → [TMJ crop] → registration

Six independent buttons: nothing in the code controls the order or the provenance of the files. The only hard constraint is the one on registration.

StepStatusWhy
MRI orientation / centeringeffectively mandatoryelastix starts with no initial transform and at a single resolution
L/R cropoptionalbilateral acquisitions; produces the left/right suffixes that the resample step reads back
Resamplemandatory in practiceMRI and CBCT must share a comparable spacing; this is also where centering happens
Approximation (auto or manual)effectively mandatoryprovides the coarse alignment elastix cannot find on its own
TMJ cropoptionalreduces the field of view
Registrationthe goalrequires CBCT + MRI + a CBCT segmentation

"Effectively mandatory" is visible in the code: ElastixReg is called with initial_transform=None and NumberOfResolutions = 1 (AREG_MRI.py:61), and process_images's error message says "PLEASE MAKE SURE THE IMAGES ARE OF SIMILAR SIZE, ORIENTATION, AND APPROXIMATED". The registration inputs also follow a strict naming convention, checked by get_corresponding_file (AREG_MRI.py:98): <ID>_CBCT*.nii.gz, <ID>_MR*.nii.gz or <ID>_MRI*.nii.gz, mask <ID>_CBCT*.nii.gz. Only .nii.gz is accepted, never .nii.

Trap

Nothing prevents launching the registration without having gone through orientation, resample and approximation first: the CLI will accept the files, and elastix will silently diverge for lack of convergence, with no message distinguishing that from a "normal" failure.

4. MRI orientation and centering

MRI2CBCT_ORIENT_CENTER_MRI.py, modify_image_properties. No resampling: a header rewrite. The voxel array is untouched, only Direction, Spacing[2] and Origin change.

  1. SetDirection(new_direction) — a 3×3 matrix built from the checkbox table, flattened row by row by getCheckboxValuesOrient (MRI2CBCT.py:847); default (0,0,-1, 1,0,0, 0,-1,0) (MRI2CBCT.py:866), the value commented "USE THIS DIRECTION FOR MRI" at the bottom of the CLI. The table enforces a single ±1 per row and per column: only the 48 signed permutation matrices are reachable.
  2. Spacing[2] is replaced by acquisition_z_spacing if Bilateral MRI is checked (default 3.0 mm, read if needed from DICOM tag 0018,0088 then 0018,0050 of the chosen node, MRI2CBCT.py:733); otherwise the string "None" is passed and the original spacing holds. This is the workaround for bilateral acquisitions whose inter-slice spacing is lost at NIfTI conversion.
  3. New origin: [(size[i]*spacing[i])/2] permuted into [o[2], -o[0], o[1]], commented "FOR MRI". This permutation is coupled to the default matrix: changing the direction without also changing it decenters the volume.

Output: <id>_OR.nii or <id>_OR.nii.gz, depending on the input extension.

Trap

The origin permutation from step 3 is only consistent with the default direction matrix. Choosing a different combination of checkboxes without revisiting this permutation decenters the volume.

5. Cropping

5.1 L/R crop — LR_crop.py

Purely geometric, sitk.RegionOfInterest, half and half.

  • MRI: cut along the Z axis (size[2]//2); lower half → _cropLeft, upper half → _cropRight.
  • CBCT and Seg: cut along the X axis, with the two halves swapped if direction[0,0] < 0, then the names are deliberately crossed (the left half is written to _cropRight and vice versa) under a # TODO: Check why left is right and why right is left (LR_crop.py:64).

Outputs in <output>/CBCT, /MRI, /Seg. The left/right suffixes are then read back by the resample step, with a plain in input_path.lower().

Trap

Left and right are deliberately swapped in crop_cbct, for no elucidated reason (the TODO confirms it right in the code itself); the whole downstream chain (mirroring at resample, side detection) then depends on these crossed names.

5.2 TMJ crop — MRI2CBCT_TMJ_CROP.py

  1. GetPatients pairs CBCT / MRI / Seg by identifier (TMJ_crop.py:24: a long chain of split calls on _Scan, _T1, _CBCT, _crop…). A segmentation never creates a patient on its own; a patient missing any of the three is skipped.
  2. segment_condyle (§6) → binary mask in the CBCT-half coordinate frame.
  3. Bounding box: MARGIN = 3 voxels, but FIXED_BBOX_VOXELS = [400,400,400] is active, so the margin is dead — the box is a 400-voxel cube centered on the mask's centroid, clamped to the volume.
  4. The 8 corners are mapped to world space via cbct_half.affine; the full CBCT and the MRI are cropped to this world-space box, each on its own grid (crop_by_world_corners goes back through each image's inverse affine).
  5. The CBCT and the segmentation are resampled onto the grid of the cropped MRI (nibabel.processing.resample_from_to, order=1 for the CBCT, order=0 for labels).

Outputs: MRI/<id>_MRI_TMJ_crop<side>.nii.gz, CBCT/<id>_CBCT_TMJ_crop<side>.nii.gz, CBCT seg/<id>_Seg_TMJ_crop<side>.nii.gz, CBCT seg/<id>_Mask_TMJ_crop<side>.nii.gz, Mask/<id>_Mask_TMJ_<side>.nii.gz (raw mask, for inspection). <id> is the first token of the CBCT filename (stem.split(".")[0].split("_")[0]). A patient is skipped if MRI/<id>_MRI_TMJ_crop*.nii.gz already exists.

The exact architecture and the nnU-Net preprocessing (intensity normalization, target spacing, patch size) live in the downloaded plans.json / dataset.json: not determinable from the repository. The code does no preprocessing of its own, it delegates everything to nnU-Net. os.environ['nnUNet_results'] is derived by walking up two levels from the trainer folder.

Preprocessing specific to the module, detect_side_and_half (condyle_segmentation.py:84): the center of mass of the MRI's non-zero voxels is compared in world coordinates to the CBCT's center (cog_w[0] > cbct_mid_w[0] → side Right). The CBCT is split in two along axis index 0, the retained half being chosen from the sign of affine[:3,0][0] — so independently of the voxel storage direction. Goal: presenting the network with the same field of view as during training.

Post-processing: computed

mask = pred if pred.max() > 1 else (pred > PROBA_THR)   # PROBA_THR = 0.02
mask = biggest_cc(mask)                                  # scipy.ndimage.label

nnUNetv2_predict writes a label map (the probabilities go into a .npz that is never read back), so pred.max() equals 1 and the branch actually taken is the 0.02 threshold, i.e. "label ≠ 0"; PROBA_THR would only have an effect on a probability map. The largest connected component is kept: the result is a single, binary condyle.

7. Resampling

MRI2CBCT_RESAMPLE_CBCT_MRI.py + resample.py. Six input folders: MRI/CBCT/Seg × T1/T2. create_csv recursively inventories .nii, .nii.gz, .nrrd, .nrrd.gz, .gipl, .gipl.gz and records the size and spacing of each.

Table defaults (MRI2CBCT.py:543): 443 × 443 × 119 slices, spacing 0.3 × 0.3 × 0.3 mm, each row can be disabled via "Keep the same size / spacing as the input scan" (the string "None" is then passed to the CLI).

Inputresample_sizeInterpolationNote
MRIthe table's valuelineariso_spacing=True actually serves as the isMRI flag
CBCTforced to "None"linearonly the spacing changes, its own size is kept
Segforced to "None"nearest neighbor (linear=False)

The forcing is commented at line 163: imposing the MRI's size on the CBCT would crop the whole skull down to the dimensions of the small MRI volume.

In resample_fn:

  • fit_spacing=True (a target size is given) → output_spacing = sp·si/o_si, the physical extent is preserved; overwritten afterward if an explicit spacing is supplied.
  • center → output_origin -= (output_physical_size − input_physical_size)/2, the offset being first multiplied by the direction matrix for the MRI.
  • Mirroring: if MRI and the name contains right and center is false, the volume is flipped along Z (sitk.Flip [False,False,True]) before resampling then flipped back afterward. The side comes from the filename produced by the L/R crop.
  • DefaultPixelValue = the input volume's minimum, not 0: padding reuses the actual background.
  • The iso_spacing == "True" branch compares a boolean to a string: dead.

Outputs in <output>/MRI, /CBCT, /Seg, names unchanged. The output path is file_path.replace(dirname(file_path), output): any input directory tree is flattened, two same-named files from different subfolders silently overwrite each other.

Trap

Flattening subfolders at resample silently overwrites two same-named files coming from different subfolders. The error handler meant to flag the issue is itself broken (§12).

8. Approximation

Two paths coexist in the same UI section, writing into the same <output>/first_approximation/ folder under the same file names.

8.1 Automatic path

The MRI2CBCT_APPROX CLI registers nothing: it produces a pair of points and a rotation, and the widget finishes the job (the CLI has no access to Slicer's modules). approximation() (approximate.py:77), per CBCT/MRI pair (folders, or a single file if both inputs are files — "Scene Volume" mode):

  1. segment_condyle → predicted mask of the condyle on the correct CBCT half;
  2. CBCT point = computed centroid of the mask's voxels, mapped to RAS world space by cbct_half.affine;
  3. MRI point = world_center_of_mass, center of mass of the whole MRI's non-zero voxels, in RAS;
  4. rotation = compute_rotation_correction (approx_utils.py:38): both images go through nib.as_closest_canonical, the 3×3 part of each affine is orthogonalized by SVD (U @ Vt), returning R_cbct @ R_mri.T. After recanonicalization the frames are already close to RAS: this rotation only catches the residual that is not axis-aligned;
  5. mri_point_rotated = R @ mri_point;
  6. writing first_approximation/points/<id>_approx_points.json: patient_id, side, cbct_point_ras, mri_point_rotated_ras, rotation_ras, mri_path, cbct_path.

Then finalizeApproximation() (Approx_MRI2CBCT.py:119), triggered from onProcessUpdate on module_name == "MRI2CBCT approximation" (MRI2CBCT.py:2166): two single-point fiducials (fixed = CBCT point, moving = rotated MRI point) are passed to Slicer's fiducialregistration module with transformType = "Translation"; the 4×4 RAS matrix is assembled (3×3 block = rotation from the JSON, column 3 = translation); the MRI volume is loaded, placed under this transform, hardenTransform, written.

In other words: rotation derived from the headers, translation derived from pairing the condyle centroid with the MRI's center of mass. The network only serves to provide the CBCT point.

8.2 Manual path

ManualApprox_MRI2CBCT.py, injected into the Approximate section by injectUI. A port of GreedyReg, entirely in the widget.

  • 6 sliders: X/Y/Z rotations (−180…180°), X/Y/Z translations (−200…200 mm). onManualTransformChanged builds a vtkTransform: Translate then RotateX/Y/Z. Since vtkTransform works in PreMultiply, the final matrix is T·Rx·Ry·Rz: the rotation happens around the origin of the RAS frame, not around the volume's center — hence the importance of the upstream centering steps.
  • "Center MRI on CBCT" adds to the translation the gap between the centers of the GetRASBounds.
  • "Enable Interactive Tool" activates Slicer's native handles, translation and rotation allowed, scaling disabled (SetEditorScalingEnabled(False)), and shows the qMRMLTransformDisplayNodeWidget of the Transforms module.
  • "Confirm & Save Alignment" clones the MRI, hardens the transform, writes the volume, then writes the .tfm by hand.
Confirmed bug

"Confirm & Save" crashes if the volume comes from the scene: onConfirm does os.path.basename(self._mri_path) while _mri_path is reset to None as soon as the volume is picked via the qMRMLNodeComboBox (ManualApprox_MRI2CBCT.py:247). Only "Load from Folders Above" works.

8.3 What approximation hands off to registration

Nothing formal: no parameter is passed to elastix. It produces an already-moved MRI volume, and that is the volume the operator must point to as the MRI folder for registration. The .tfm is never read back by the module.

9. Normalization

A 4-column × 2-row table (MRI, CBCT): Normalization Min/Max, Percentile Min/Max. Two sets of defaults, DefaultNorm (MRI2CBCT.py:1157):

PresetMRI [min, max, p_low, p_high]CBCT [min, max, p_low, p_high]
Default 1 (applied on open)[0, 100, 0, 100][0, 75, 10, 95]
Default 2[0, 100, 10, 95][0, 100, 10, 95]

The two roles are distinct (normalize_percentile.py):

  • the percentiles define the intensity window. np.percentile is computed over the whole array, background included — not on the non-zero voxels. On a CBCT mostly made of air, p10 falls in the background and p95 clips the densest structures.
  • min_norm / max_norm define the output range:
normalized = np.clip((array - lo) / (hi - lo), 0, 1)
scaled     = normalized * (max_norm - min_norm) + min_norm

With Default 1, the MRI is windowed on percentiles [0, 100] — so a pure min–max normalization with no clipping, rescaled to [0, 100] — while the CBCT is windowed between its p10 and its p95 then rescaled to [0, 75], deliberately lower than the MRI. With Default 2 both modalities receive the same treatment.

There is only one normalization method in the code: no choice of algorithm, just these four numbers per modality. CheckNormalization (Reg_MRI2CBCT.py:54) only checks max > min on both pairs. normalize() only handles .nii.gz files (a .nii is silently skipped) and writes <name>_percentile=[lo,hi]_norm=[min,max].nii.gz: filenames grow at every step, brackets and commas included.

10. Registration

MRI2CBCT_REG.py chains 6 steps, each in its own subfolder of folder_general:

#StepOutput
1MRI intensity inversiona01_MRI_inv/
2MRI normalizationa2_MRI_inv_norm/percentile=[…]_norm=[…]/
3masking the MRI with the CBCT segmentationa3_MRI_inv_norm_mask/…
4CBCT normalizationb2_CBCT_norm/…
5masking the CBCT with the same segmentationb3_CBCT_norm_mask_l2/…
6elastixmri=inv+norm[…]+p[…]_cbct=norm[…]+p[…]+mask/

Inversion (mri_inverse.py:19): inverted = max_intensity − array, then the voxels that were 0 are reset to 0. Bone, dark on MRI, becomes bright as on CBCT: the intensity relationship between the two modalities becomes roughly monotonic. Suffix _inv, os.listdir so non-recursive.

Masking (apply_mask.py): the two modalities are masked by the same file, the CBCT segmentation (cbct_label2), with seg_label = 1. If the value 1 is present, the segmentation is binarized as array == 1; otherwise the mask is used as-is. A mask of a different size is resampled onto the image's grid with nearest neighbor. A file whose name contains neither _CBCT nor _MR is skipped. The result is cast to sitk.sitkInt16: the floating-point normalized values are truncated to integers — only 76 distinct levels remain for a CBCT normalized on [0, 75].

Confirmed bug (fixed since, see §14)

12. Traps and fragile spots

Confirmed bug

install_function() always fails. The dependency list (MRI2CBCT.py:96) contains ('nnunet_version', "==2.8.0"); that package does not exist on PyPI (AMASSS correctly declares ('nnunetv2','2.8.0')). check_lib_installed therefore always returns False: an install dialog on every action, a pip_install that raises, errorDisplay, and a False return — a value every caller ignores, so the action proceeds anyway.

Confirmed bug

Elastix's center of rotation is lost. MatrixRetrieval reads TransformParameters but not the CenterOfRotationPoint that the output parameter map also carries; the reconstructed sitk.Euler3DTransform therefore rotates around the origin. The written and applied transform only coincides with the one estimated by elastix if that center is at the origin. Code copied verbatim from AREG_CBCT/AREG_CBCT_utils/utils.py:830: the problem is shared.

Trap

Two .tfm conventions for the same step. Manual approximation writes flip @ M @ flip with no inversion (ManualApprox_MRI2CBCT.py:472), where FlexReg writes inv(flip @ M @ flip) for the same conversion — the ITK convention being fixed → moving. The automatic path goes through slicer.util.saveNode, which applies the correct convention.

Confirmed bug

"Confirm & Save" crashes if the volume comes from the scene: onConfirm does os.path.basename(self._mri_path) while _mri_path is reset to None as soon as the volume is picked via the qMRMLNodeComboBox (ManualApprox_MRI2CBCT.py:247). Only "Load from Folders Above" works.

Confirmed bug

The resample's error handler crashes too: logger.warning(e, file=sys.stderr) (resample.py:240) — Logger.warning does not accept file=, so the caught exception turns into an uncaught TypeError.

Confirmed bug

T2 without T1 breaks the resample: mri_output_folder / cbct_output_folder are only defined in the T1 branch (MRI2CBCT_RESAMPLE_CBCT_MRI.py:157) → NameError.

Trap

Hardcoded _T1/_T2 suffixes in extract_patient_id (TMJ_crop.py:24) and GetPatients (utils_CBCT.py:41): a _T3 breaks the pairing. Note shared with AREG_CBCT.

Trap

Left and right deliberately swapped in crop_cbct, for no elucidated reason (TODO); the whole downstream chain (mirroring at resample, side detection) depends on these names.

Trap

The orientation's origin permutation is written for the default matrix: a different direction in the table decenters the volume.

Trap

Subfolder flattening at resample: same-named files overwritten with no message.

14. Literature

The corresponding paper is Novel CBCT-MRI Registration Approach for Enhanced Analysis of Temporomandibular Degenerative Joint Disease, Leroux G. et al., Clinical Image-Based Procedures (CLIP 2024), LNCS vol. 15196, Springer. Its abstract describes the sequence implemented here: automatic orientation, resampling, MRI inversion, normalization, then rigid registration by mutual information.

Two divergences between the paper and the current state of the code: the repository's approximation at the time was the torchreg + NMI path of crop_approximation.py, since replaced by nnU-Net condyle + fiducialregistration; and neither the condyle segmentation nor the TMJ crop appear in the published abstract. The repository's README, for that matter, still states "Currently, cropping is done using the AutoCrop method. In the future, this step will be replaced by a trained model" — that has since been done.

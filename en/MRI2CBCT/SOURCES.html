MRI2CBCT — sources

What the module owes to the literature, what was retrieved in full text, and what remains paywalled or untraceable.

bibliography Pipeline page

Partially published, and published out of step with the code. Two texts do bear on this module: the method paper (Leroux et al., CLIP 2024, paywalled) and a clinical case series that documents its use (Caleme et al., Seminars in Orthodontics 2025, retrieved here). Neither describes the version that runs today: the CLIP 2024 paper describes the torchreg + NMI approximation, since replaced by an nnU-Net segmentation of the condyle followed by a single-point fiducialregistration; the 2025 case series refers back to the CLIP paper for technical detail and mentions neither the condyle segmentation nor the TMJ crop. A third text (Gaydamour et al., CLIP 2025, paywalled) reports in passing the only numerical evaluation of the pipeline published to date: sub-millimeter accuracy, 98.75% success rate.

Verified

What is published and confirmed in the code: the final registration is indeed a rigid elastix using Mattes mutual information.

Neighboring work

Related works that do not describe this module.

  • Al-Saleh 2016 and 2017 (retrieved above): a team from the University of Alberta, unaffiliated with DCBIA. They frame the problem — MRI/CBCT registration of the TMJ, its pitfalls, the state of the art before this module — and serve as a point of comparison, not a description of the code.
  • elastix (Klein 2010, retrieved): describes the registration library, not its use here. The module goes through the Python ITKElastix wrapper (itk.ElastixRegistrationMethod) with the default rigid parameter map, a single resolution and no initial transform — a parameterization detail the paper obviously does not cover. Second usual citation for elastix, not retrieved here: Shamonin D.P. et al., Fast parallel image registration on CPU and GPU…, Front. Neuroinform. 7:50, 2014 (10.3389/fninf.2013.00050, CC-BY).
  • nnU-Net (Isensee, retrieved): the self-configuring method behind the condyle segmentation network. Neither the training, nor the dataset, nor the evaluation of the Dataset001_myseg weights used by MRI2CBCT is documented anywhere, publication included.
  • Upstream modules of the extension, cited by Caleme 2025 as input preparation but never called from MRI2CBCT's code (ASO, AREG, AMASSS — mentioned here as bibliographic neighbors, not as code dependencies): ASO/AREG — Anchling L. et al., Automated Orientation and Registration of Cone-Beam Computed Tomography Scans, CLIP 2023, LNCS (PMC11104011); AMASSS — Gillot M. et al., PLOS ONE 17(9):e0275033, 2022 (10.1371/journal.pone.0275033).
  • TorchReg (github.com/codingfisch/torchreg): the approximation path of the CLIP 2024 paper and the PW43 report, since abandoned in the code. No publication.

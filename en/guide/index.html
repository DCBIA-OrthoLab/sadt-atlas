User guide

What each tool does, what to feed it, what it returns — without going into the code. For the internal mechanisms, switch to the Atlas.

Registration

FlexRegRegister two intraoral scans by picking the stable area yourself AREG_IOSRegister two scans of the same patient, with nothing to draw AREG_CBCTSuperimpose two CBCT scans of the same patient taken at two dates AREG_IOSCBCTPlace an intraoral scan into the frame of a CBCT GreedyRegRegister two CBCT scans using the ITK-SNAP engine MRI2CBCTAlign an MRI and a CBCT of the temporomandibular joint

Segmentation

AMASSSSegment mandible, maxilla and other structures from a CBCT BatchDentalSegSegment teeth and bone across a series of scans in one run

Landmarks & orientation

ALIPlace anatomical landmarks automatically ASOBring scans into a shared orientation

Analysis

VFACEMeasure and classify a patient's facial asymmetry DOCShapeAXIClassify 3D shapes and see what drove the decision CLICFind and classify impacted canines on a CBCT SurgMovPredEstimate the expected bone movement after surgery

Utilities

AutoCrop3DCrop a series of volumes to the same region AutoMatrixApply registration matrices across a whole series

Text & language

CNEPull structured information out of clinical notes MedXSummarise clinical reports and build a dashboard from them MedicalDataAnonymizerStrip personal data from a text document AgentAsk in plain language which tool to run
